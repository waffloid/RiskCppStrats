\textbf{Benchmark.} Full-game strength, the ground-truth metric every other
section proxies. All registered models compete except \code{v2\_knapsack} and
\code{v3}, which are code-identical to \code{v4}. Each game is three-player on
a fresh Poisson map: one model plays \emph{solo} against two copies of its
opponent, and scores 1 if it holds strictly more nodes when the game ends
(elimination or 5000 ticks), $\tfrac12$ on a tie. The 1-vs-2 protocol is
deliberately hostile: surviving a coordinated majority is a stronger signal
than a symmetric duel at these game counts. Every unordered pair plays a fixed
number of games, half solo on each side, with deterministic seeds.

Ratings are fit by maximum likelihood under a Bradley--Terry model
\citep{bradleyterry1952} via minorization--maximization \citep{hunter2004} on
the aggregate score matrix, then mapped onto the familiar Elo scale
($1500 + 400\log_{10} p$) \citep{elo1978}. Unlike sequential K-factor updates,
the fit is independent of game order and exactly reproducible from
\code{raw/games.csv}.

\textbf{Reading.} Four headlines, to be read in tiers (with four games per
pair, gaps under $\sim$100 Elo are noise; the extreme negative ratings at the
bottom are Bradley--Terry scale artifacts of near-zero score rates, not
meaningful magnitudes).

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{\code{v8} tops the ladder} (score rate 0.90) --- and \code{v8}
    is the \emph{only} model using the distance-decayed local softmax, a
    branch the lineage abandoned when v9 pivoted to OT transport. The single
    most valuable tweak tested is currently unused by every later model;
    combining \code{v8}'s softmax with \code{v12}'s transport is the most
    obvious unexplored bot.
  \item \textbf{OT transport is the strongest lineage trunk}: \code{v9},
    \code{v12}, \code{v10} fill ranks 2--5 and all clear every pre-OT model
    except \code{v8}.
  \item \textbf{\code{v11} is a regression, not noise} (0.17, adrift from both
    its parent \code{v10} at 0.65 and its child \code{v12} at 0.75). The only
    delta is effective-troops demand netting; the natural suspect is
    demand starvation --- in-transit troops cancel demand before they arrive,
    so targets stop pulling reinforcements --- which \code{v12}'s standing
    frontline-garrison demand would mask. This wants a diagnosis before the
    netting is trusted anywhere.
  \item \textbf{Pacifism is underrated at these game counts}:
    \code{v0\_expansion}, which has no war agent at all, sits mid-table at
    0.60, above \code{v4}--\code{v6}. In the 1-vs-2 protocol, not initiating
    fights while out-expanding is evidently a viable strategy; the knapsack-era
    aggressors (\code{v1\_knapsack}, hybrid) are the losers of the entire
    tournament.
\end{enumerate}

The raw game log and manifest under \code{results/elo/} suffice to re-fit or
extend the ladder without re-playing anything.
